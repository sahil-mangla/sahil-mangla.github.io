Privacy policy: founder-outreach

Last updated: 2 November 2026

founder-outreach is a personal tool. Its only user is Sahil Mangla, who signs in with his own Google account.

What it accesses

  • Gmail: sends emails that Sahil has approved, from his own account, and reads the metadata of those email threads (sender and message id) to detect replies and bounces.
  • Google Sheets: reads and updates one spreadsheet owned by Sahil that lists the contacts and drafts.

What it does with it

The data is used only to send, follow up on and track Sahil's own outreach emails. It is processed on Sahil's own computer and stored only in his Gmail and Google Sheet. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
