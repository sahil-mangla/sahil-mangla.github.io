founder-outreach

founder-outreach is a personal tool used by Sahil Mangla, a computer science student at Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology, to email startup founders about internship opportunities.

It runs only on Sahil's own computer. It sends emails that Sahil has written and approved from his own Gmail account, follows up on them, and keeps track of replies in his own Google Sheet. It is not offered to anyone else.
